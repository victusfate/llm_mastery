# Choose your tutor interface

Every concept popup and lesson offers Discuss with my tutor. The generated prompt carries the current topic and course explanation, requests definitions and visual examples, and asks the tutor to adapt one question at a time. You choose how deeply to explore.

## Three steps

1. Choose ChatGPT, Claude, OpenRouter, or a local/other chat interface.
2. Select Prepare & copy prompt. The text stays visible if clipboard access is blocked.
3. Open your tutor and paste the prompt. Return to the course for a fresh unaided check and hands-on evidence.

The course has no model runtime, paid API dependency, account-key storage, or Chrome-specific integration. It works in ordinary browsers. Your chosen provider's account and usage conditions still apply. Prompts include the course explanation; personal notes are not added automatically.

For Ollama, enter the URL of a chat interface such as Open WebUI. Ollama's API port is not itself a chat webpage. Native desktop chat apps can also receive the copied prompt manually.

## Free-model options

Checked September 8, 2026. Free model access and quotas change; check the provider before depending on it for a study schedule.

| Option | How to use it here | Limits to consider |
| --- | --- | --- |

Google's [API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) distinguishes free and paid access and their data-use terms. Groq documents [rate limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits). OpenRouter documents [limits](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/limits). This course does not promise free, fast, unlimited hosted tutoring.

## Confidence and generated feedback

Ask for hints and explanations, then close the conversation for an unaided check. Models can produce incorrect explanations, computations, and references. Verify against the runnable examples and primary readings. Mark assistance in course assessments; a model's encouragement does not award practical mastery.
